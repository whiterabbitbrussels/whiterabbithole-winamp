02 · The reality

The reality for
independent music creators.

Platform fragmentation

Your music lives on Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, YouTube, SoundCloud. No single place to build your audience or control your narrative. Revenue scattered across 5+ dashboards.

Monetization complexity

Streaming royalties don't pay the bills. You're juggling Patreon, Discord, merch, fan DMs. No unified system. You're losing money to the middleman every step.

Audience ownership

Your fans are on platforms you don't control. Algorithm changes = audience disappears overnight. You have zero direct relationship with your listeners.
